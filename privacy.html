Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: August 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name (optional)
  • Authentication credentials (stored securely via Supabase Auth)
  • GitHub profile information (if you sign up with GitHub)

1.2 Pod and Habit Data

We collect information related to your pod participation:

  • Pod names and descriptions
  • Habit names and descriptions
  • Check-in status (done, missed, waiting)
  • Check-in dates
  • Pod membership and role (admin/member)

1.3 Usage Data

We may collect information about how you use the service, including:

  • Pages visited and features used
  • Time spent on the service
  • Device information (browser type, operating system)
  • IP address and general location information
  • Referral sources and navigation paths

1.4 Email Communications

We collect email addresses for the purpose of sending:

  • Account verification and authentication emails
  • Pod invitation notifications
  • Login security alerts
  • Pod deletion confirmations
  • Password reset emails
  • Streak notifications (when pod members miss check-ins)
  • Inactive user reminders (when users haven't checked in for 7+ days)
  • Marketing communications (optional, user-controlled)

You have control over your email preferences through our Email Preferences settings. By default, marketing communications are enabled, but you may opt out at any time. Pod activity notifications (including streak notifications and inactive user reminders) are controlled by the Marketing Communications toggle, as these are considered promotional in nature. Essential account emails (verification, security, password reset) cannot be disabled.

1.5 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain sessions
  • Remember user preferences and settings
  • Analyze service usage patterns
  • Improve service performance and user experience

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Knot service
  • Process your check-ins and display them to your pod members
  • Send you notifications about pod activity (if enabled via Marketing Communications toggle)
  • Authenticate your account and secure your data
  • Improve our service and develop new features
  • Communicate with you about service updates
  • Send welcome emails after account creation
  • Send password reset emails when requested
  • Send streak notifications when pod members miss check-ins (if Marketing Communications enabled)
  • Send inactive user reminders when you haven't checked in for 7+ days (if Marketing Communications enabled)

2.1 Email Preferences Control

You have full control over your email communication preferences through our Email Preferences settings page. By default, Marketing Communications are enabled, which includes pod activity notifications (streak notifications and inactive user reminders). You may opt out of Marketing Communications at any time by toggling the setting off in your Email Preferences page. This change will take effect immediately and will prevent future pod activity emails from being sent to you.

Essential account emails (account verification, security alerts, password reset emails, account deletion confirmations) cannot be disabled as they are necessary for account security and operation. These emails will always be sent regardless of your Marketing Communications preference.

Your email preference status is stored as part of your user metadata in Supabase Auth and is respected by our email sending functions to ensure your preferences are honored.

3. Information Sharing

3.1 Within Your Pod

Your check-in status, habit commitments, and display name are visible to other members of your pod. This is a core feature of the service for accountability purposes.

3.2 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to provide Knot:

Supabase

We use Supabase for:

  • Authentication and user management
  • Database storage and management
  • Real-time data synchronization
  • Row-level security and access control

Supabase processes your data in accordance with their own privacy policy. You can review the Supabase Privacy Policy for more information about how they handle your data.

EmailJS

We use EmailJS for sending transactional and promotional emails, including:

  • Welcome emails after account creation
  • Pod invitation emails
  • Login security notifications
  • Pod deletion confirmations
  • Password reset emails (via Supabase Auth)
  • Streak notifications (when pod members miss check-ins)
  • Inactive user reminders (when users haven't checked in for 7+ days)
  • Marketing communications (if enabled by user)

EmailJS processes email content and recipient information to deliver these emails. We send emails through EmailJS using our service account credentials. You can review the EmailJS Privacy Policy for more information about their data practices.

Custom Knot Captcha System

We use a custom-built captcha system ("Knot Captcha") for bot prevention and security verification. This system operates entirely within your browser and does not send any personal information to external captcha services. The captcha challenge involves an interactive drag-to-verify task that is processed locally. No captcha data is transmitted to third-party services or stored on our servers beyond the verification result (success/failure).

GTranslate (Translation Service)

We use GTranslate to provide website translation functionality, allowing users to translate our content into multiple languages for better accessibility. GTranslate processes page content to provide translated versions of our website. We use this service to make our platform accessible to users who prefer to read content in languages other than English. You can review the GTranslate Privacy Policy for more information about their data practices.

UNGH (Version Checking Service)

We use UNGH to check for application updates by fetching repository metadata and commit information. This service operates anonymously without requiring authentication or API keys, and it does not collect or process any personal information. UNGH only retrieves public repository data to determine if new versions of the application are available. You can review the UNGH website for more information about their service.

We share only the minimum necessary data with these services to provide their functionality. Each service has its own privacy policy which governs their use of your data.

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users or others.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Encryption of data at rest through Supabase's security infrastructure
  • Secure authentication via Supabase Auth with password hashing
  • Row-level security (RLS) policies to control data access
  • Regular security reviews and updates
  • Access controls to limit who can view your data
  • Secure session management with token-based authentication
  • Input validation and sanitization to prevent injection attacks

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage users to use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication when available.

4.1 Account Switching and Session Management

Knot provides an account switching feature that allows you to manage multiple accounts. To enable instant switching between accounts without requiring repeated authentication, we store session tokens (access tokens and refresh tokens) in your browser's localStorage. This includes:

  • Access tokens for immediate authentication
  • Refresh tokens for obtaining new access tokens
  • Token expiration timestamps
  • Account profile information (name, email, avatar)

Security Notice: Storing authentication tokens in localStorage is vulnerable to cross-site scripting (XSS) attacks. If malicious code is executed on your device, these tokens could potentially be accessed. We implement this approach for user convenience, but you should be aware of this security trade-off. For enhanced security, consider using a dedicated browser profile or clearing your localStorage after each session if you share your device.

Session tokens are automatically refreshed by Supabase when they expire, and the updated tokens are stored in localStorage. When you remove an account from the account switcher, the associated session tokens are deleted from localStorage. When you log out of an account, the Supabase session is revoked and the tokens are removed.

4.2 Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that compromises your personal information, we will notify affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Notifications will include information about what happened, what data was affected, and steps you can take to protect yourself.

5. Data Retention

5.1 Account Data

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. When you request account deletion, we implement a two-stage deletion process:

  • Stage 1 - Soft Deletion: Your account data is marked as deleted in our database using soft deletion flags. This includes your user profile, pod memberships, check-ins, and associated data. This data remains in our database for a period of 30 days to allow for recovery if needed.
  • Stage 2 - Hard Deletion: Simultaneously with soft deletion, we permanently delete your authentication credentials from Supabase Auth, rendering your account inaccessible. This hard deletion is irreversible.

After the 30-day recovery period, soft-deleted data may be permanently removed from our systems, except as required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as security auditing and compliance.

5.2 Pod and Check-in Data

Pod and check-in data is retained to maintain the accountability history of your pod. If you leave a pod, your historical check-in data remains visible to pod members for transparency and accountability purposes. When you delete your account, your check-in data is soft-deleted as described above. You may request immediate deletion of this data by contacting us, but this may affect the integrity of your pod's tracking history.

5.3 Email Logs

Email delivery logs are retained for up to 90 days for troubleshooting and security purposes. Email content is not stored beyond what is necessary for delivery through EmailJS. We track which types of emails have been sent to prevent duplicate notifications, but this tracking data does not include email content.

5.4 Security Logs

Security logs, including login attempts, authentication events, and account switching activities, are retained for up to 180 days for security monitoring and incident response purposes.

5.5 Email Preference Data

Your email communication preferences (marketing opt-out status) are stored as part of your user metadata in Supabase Auth. This data is retained for as long as your account exists and is deleted when your account is permanently deleted.
