About MyID

A secure, passwordless way to sign in to your account

What is MyID?

MyID is our name for passkeys - a modern authentication method that replaces traditional passwords with your device's built-in security features. We renamed it to MyID to make it more personal and easier to understand.

When you use MyID, you sign in using biometric authentication (fingerprint, face recognition) or a PIN, making it both more secure and more convenient than passwords.

How It Works

MyID uses public-key cryptography to authenticate you without ever sending your password over the internet:

  • Registration: When you add a MyID, your device creates a unique cryptographic key pair. The private key never leaves your device.
  • Authentication: When you sign in, your device proves you have the private key using biometrics or PIN.
  • Verification: The server verifies your identity using the public key, without ever seeing your private key.

Benefits

  • More Secure: No passwords to remember, guess, or steal. Your private key never leaves your device.
  • Phishing Resistant: MyID can't be tricked by fake websites - it only works with the legitimate site.
  • Convenient: Sign in instantly with Face ID, Touch ID, or Windows Hello - no typing required.
  • Device-Based: Each device has its own MyID. You can add multiple devices to your account.

Device Support

MyID is supported on most modern devices and browsers:

  • macOS: Safari, Chrome, Edge (with Touch ID or Apple Watch)
  • iOS: Safari (with Face ID or Touch ID)
  • Windows: Chrome, Edge (with Windows Hello)
  • Android: Chrome, Edge (with fingerprint or face unlock)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MyID the same as a password?
No. MyID uses cryptographic keys instead of passwords. You don't need to remember anything - your device handles the authentication.

What happens if I lose my device?
If you lose a device with a MyID, you can delete it from your account settings and add a new MyID on another device. As long as you have another login method (password, Google, GitHub), you can still access your account.

Can I use MyID on multiple devices?
Yes! You can add a MyID on each of your devices. Each device gets its own unique MyID.

Is MyID private?
Yes. Your biometric data never leaves your device. The authentication happens locally, and only a cryptographic proof is sent to the server.
